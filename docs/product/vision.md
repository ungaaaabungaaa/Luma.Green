# Vision

> **Status:** agreed with the founder, 29 Sep 2026. Raw source:
> [brief.md](./brief.md).

## The problem

Recyclable material in an Indian city passes through many hands: the household,
the kabadiwala, one or more yards, a recycler and finally a manufacturer. Every
hand-off is informal. Prices are opaque, weights are guessed, nothing is written
down, and nobody can prove where a bale of material came from.

## What Luma.Green is

The infrastructure that connects the whole chain on one platform.

- **Households** sell scrap in three taps: photo, booking, paid at the door.
- **Kabadiwalas** get pickups without walking the streets, and see what yards
  pay today.
- **Preprocessors (yards)** see what nearby kabadiwalas hold and book
  collections.
- **Recyclers** source sorted feedstock; **manufacturers** see what recyclers
  produce and order it.
- **Saathis** — people who want quick work — do pickups, sorting and loading for
  any of them.

On the platform everyone can exchange material, see the latest prices and,
later, pay each other through escrow.

## Principles

1. **Record every hand-off.** Material is re-sorted at each step and its name
   and price change. The platform keeps the chain — who, what, how much, at what
   price — so it can be traced later.
2. **Simple enough for a first smartphone.** Icons, big buttons, numbers and the
   user's own language. Mobile first; desktop only where the job needs it.
3. **Every language is first-class.** A hand-translated interface in 12
   languages; Kannada, Hindi and English lead for the Bengaluru pilot.
4. **Verified participants only.** A person checks every business and every
   Saathi before they can trade.
5. **Built to last, tuned by data.** Laws and the trade change slowly, so once
   the core is right it should run for years. We launch small, measure
   everything and streamline from real use.

## Scope

| Now — pilot, October 2026                               | Next                                    | Later                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Onboarding and manual verification for every role       | Kabadiwala → yard collections           | Escrow payments between businesses     |
| Household: photo → estimate → pickup → paid at the door | Recycler ↔ manufacturer orders          | Carbon credits and carbon trading      |
| Kabadiwala: requests, auto-accept, weigh and record     | WhatsApp channel for households         | Solar panel applications               |
| Prices: own rates, a minimum table and a fallback table | Notifications over WhatsApp             | Documentation and legal services       |
| One admin console                                       | Saathi jobs and their payment structure | Machinery data bank and other services |

## What the pilot has to prove

Real households in Bengaluru book pickups; kabadiwalas accept and complete them;
every pickup is weighed and recorded; and we learn exactly where the flow
breaks. Targets and measures are in [delivery/pilot.md](../delivery/pilot.md).
