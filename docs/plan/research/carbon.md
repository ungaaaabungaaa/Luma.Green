# carbon

_Research brief, 29 September 2026. Headline:_ India's compliance carbon market (CCTS) is expected to see its first trades around October 2026, but none of its 8 approved offset methodologies credits recycling. That leaves voluntary plastic credits as Luma's realistic credit route: 1 credit = 1 tonne of plastic, selling for $106–$804 on PCX, against a 2024 voluntary carbon average of $6.34/tCO2e. Every route needs per-lot dry weights from calibrated scales, invoices and receiver GRNs, and an unbroken chain of custody recorded from day one.

### Credit-ready ledger: fields to capture per transaction

**Transactions:**

- **T1** household pickup or drop-off (household → kabadiwala)
- **T2** dispatch between businesses (kabadiwala → yard → recycler → manufacturer)
- **T3** sorting run at a yard
- **T4** processing batch at a recycler
- **M** master data, captured once per org or person and referenced by every transaction

**Standards:**

- **V-PC** Verra PWRM0001 v1.1 (plastic collection)
- **V-PR** Verra PWRM0002 v1.1 (plastic recycling)
- **V-PS** Verra Plastic Standard v1.0
- **V-7** Verra VMR0007 with CDM AMS-III.AJ (carbon credits for HDPE, LDPE, PET, PVC, PP, container glass, aluminium, steel)
- **GS448** Gold Standard 448 v1.0 (carbon credits for metals, alloys, minerals)
- **PCX** PCX Plastic Pollution Reduction Standard v8
- **ICM** India's CCTS offset mechanism
- **SWM26** Solid Waste Management Rules 2026 (CPCB portal)

**Notes**

- Credits are counted at dispatch (T2) on a calibrated scale. Doorstep weights (half-kilo steps today) pay households and do not need to meet MRV standards.
- MRV exports never include a household's name, phone, exact address or photos. Household photos keep their 90-day deletion.
- Nothing above is a credit until a registry issues a serial number. Until then, show "estimated" and "credit-ready" quantities only.

## Recommendations

- **Lot passport with a credit-readiness checklist (investor demo)** (prototype-now): Each seeded demo lot shows its journey household → kabadiwala → yard → recycler on a map. Alongside: weights, dates, weigh-slip photos, invoice and GRN, plus a checklist of the evidence each standard needs (Verra plastic, PCX, GS 448) and what is missing. This is what a verifier samples: PWRM0001 dispatch weights cross-checked with invoices and GRNs, and PCX chain-of-custody completeness. It makes 'credits later' a visible asset without claiming any credits now.
- **Honest impact counter that separates estimated, credit-ready and issued** (prototype-now): Replace the v1 idea of platform-minted carbonCredits (convex/schema.ts) with three numbers. First, kg diverted by material. Second, indicative tCO2e from a dated, sourced, admin-editable factor table, frozen per lot the way prices are. Third, credit-ready tonnes, meaning lots with complete evidence. Only registry serials (Verra, GS, PCX, or BEE via Grid-India) are ever called credits. This keeps the investor story accurate and avoids exposure to greenwashing complaints.
- **Green-bonus preview for kabadiwalas and Saathis** (prototype-now): Show the revenue-share rule as an effective-dated admin table, like the price floors. It splits net credit revenue between kabadiwala, Saathi, household points and Luma. Add an illustrative per-kg bonus at low and high prices ($106–$804/t), clearly marked 'not yet earned'. Verra and PCX reward visibly higher collector income, and Plastic Bank's cash-plus-bonus model fits Luma's UPI flow. This preview is also how pilot users will be persuaded to assign their credit rights.
- **Append-only lots and movements with two-sided receipts and a sorting mass balance** (pilot): The sender records the weight at dispatch; the receiver confirms its own weight (GRN) and the invoice. Yard sorting runs record input lots → graded output lots plus reject weight. Differences above a tolerance go to the admin. This meets PWRM0001/0002 monitoring, reuses the planned inventoryMovements with sort_out/sort_in, and blocks double claims.
- **Scale registry and weigh-slip capture at dispatch** (pilot): Each org lists its scales: ID, type, capacity, and Legal Metrology stamping or calibration date. Each dispatch picks a scale and attaches a photo of the display or weighbridge ticket, and lots weighed on out-of-date scales are marked not credit-ready. Verra requires calibration at least every 3 years, and PCX requires a weighing-equipment identifier. Because PWRM0001 allows measuring at dispatch, doorstep half-kilo weighing can stay as it is.
- **Credit rights, revenue share and a baseline declaration in onboarding** (pilot): Add a versioned clause in which each kabadiwala, yard, recycler and Saathi assigns the environmental attributes of Luma-recorded volume to Luma for pooled registration, in return for the published share. Add a declaration of the last 12–36 months' volume by material. Verra needs enforceable ownership evidence held from each instance's start date, and sets the capacity-addition baseline from the prior 3 years. Without these at pilot start, the pilot volume is lost for crediting.
- **One claim per lot, locked across EPR certificates, plastic credits and carbon credits** (pilot): Give each lot a claim status: none, EPR certificate number, reserved for a program, registry serial, or retired. ICM forbids concurrent registration with another carbon market, PCX credits transfer only once, and PWRM0001 treats materials under mandatory EPR as non-additional. India's rules are silent on double counting across instruments, so Luma has to enforce it itself.
- **Privacy-safe MRV evidence store with long retention** (pilot): In any MRV record, keep households pseudonymous and their location at ward or geohash-6 level. Keep weigh slips, invoices, GRNs, bale photos and SOP versions for at least 2 years after the crediting period (up to about 23 years). Store them apart from household photos, which keep their 90-day deletion. This meets Verra's records rules and DPDP-style data minimisation.
- **Work and pay log for Saathis and kabadiwala staff** (pilot): For each job, record who worked, hours, kg handled, pay, age-18 confirmation, and PPE and training. This is the evidence for Verra §3.14 safeguards: regional prevailing wage, a living-wage aim, no withheld wages, paid overtime and under-18 limits. It also meets PCX's income criteria and gives the payout base for revenue sharing.
- **Bengaluru grouped plastic-credit project, alone or with a partner developer** (scale): Register the kabadiwala and yard network as one Verra Plastic Program grouped project: PWRM0001 for collection, PWRM0002 at partner recyclers, with each org added as an instance. Choose the start date deliberately, since validation must follow within 2 years. Consider partnering with an experienced Bengaluru player such as Hasiru Dala Innovations, which already sells traceable plastic, to reach first issuance faster. For plastics, per-tonne plastic credits pay far more than carbon credits.
- **Carbon-credit track for metals and for CCTS buyers** (scale): Use the captured transport, energy and quality data to quantify carbon under GS 448 (metals) or VMR0007 (plastics, glass, aluminium, steel). Give obligated buyers (pulp & paper, aluminium including secondary, textiles, petrochemicals) documentation packs for their recycled inputs. Watch BEE for a recycling methodology in the Phase I waste sector.
- **Export to CPCB's SWM 2026 portal and the EPR portals from the same ledger** (scale): The SWM Rules 2026 portal, due around 1 Oct 2026, tracks collection, transport and processing. Feeding both compliance reports and verifier data rooms from one ledger avoids double entry, and makes Luma hard for yards and recyclers to leave.

## Risks

- There is no Indian compliance route for recycling yet. None of the 8 ICM offset methodologies covers material recovery, offsets cannot be used for compliance, and CCC prices have not been discovered. Any 'Indian carbon credit' pitch for recycling is speculative until BEE adds a methodology.
- Plastic-credit demand is thin and contested. Verra's program shows about 40,000 credits across 40+ projects worldwide, PCX prices span $106–$804/t, and PCX has paused 'net zero plastic' claims. Brand demand could stall.
- Double counting: the same tonne could earn a CPCB EPR certificate, a plastic credit and a carbon credit. Under Verra, mandatory EPR can also make plastic collection non-additional.
- Calling platform estimates 'credits', as the v1 carbonCredits table does, could mislead investors and buyers and draw misrepresentation or greenwashing complaints. This still needs checking against India's consumer-protection guidance on environmental claims.
- Weight fraud would sink a verification: inflated weights, re-weighing the same bale, fake GRNs or uncalibrated scales. The defences are two-sided receipts, mass balance, calibration checks and anomaly alerts.
- Privacy: keeping household GPS, addresses or photos for the long MRV retention period conflicts with DPDP minimisation and Luma's 90-day photo rule. Households have to stay out of MRV records.
- Timing: Verra plastic projects must validate within 2 years of the declared start date, and ICM projects need a start on or after 1 Jan 2025. Choosing the wrong start date or missing the window forfeits early volume.
- Social risk: if credit revenue stays with Luma or the yards instead of reaching kabadiwalas and Saathis, projects fail Verra's safeguards and the impact story collapses. SWM 2026 registration may also shut out unregistered informal collectors.
- Cost: verifier audits and registry fees are largely fixed per verification. A handful of pilot testers will not produce enough tonnage to pay for them; quotes are needed.

## Open questions

- Will BEE add a material-recovery or recycling methodology to the ICM offset mechanism (waste is a Phase I sector), and when?
- What floor and forbearance prices will CERC approve for CCCs, and will offset CCCs ever count toward compliance?
- Should Luma be the project proponent of a Verra grouped plastic project, or partner with an existing developer such as Hasiru Dala Innovations in Bengaluru?
- What share of net credit revenue will kabadiwalas, yards and Saathis accept for assigning their credit rights, and should it be paid per kg after issuance or advanced earlier?
- Can a tonne that generated a CPCB EPR certificate also earn a Verra WRC or WCC? This needs a legal opinion and a verifier's view.
- Is paper or cardboard creditable under any active methodology? VMR0007 and GS 448, as read here, do not cover it.
- What does a verification cost in India, and how many tonnes a year does it take for plastic credits to pay?
- What will CPCB's SWM 2026 portal require of informal aggregators such as kabadiwalas, and can Luma report on their behalf?
- Will a verifier accept ward- or geohash-level household location as evidence of an identifiable source?
