# industry-integration

_Research brief, 29 September 2026. Headline:_ One yard-to-factory truckload already needs about six documents (purchase order, weighbridge slip, quality report, receipt note, GST invoice, e-way bill), kept across Tally, WhatsApp and paper. A CPCB audit also found a Karnataka recycler that issued recycling certificates for 3.48 lakh tonnes while its plant was not running. So Luma.Green should record each hand-off once, as evidence, and export it in Tally and CPCB formats, not try to replace factory software.

## Recommendations

- **Trade document pack: one timeline per trade (PO number, weigh-in and weigh-out slip photos, quality result, receipt/GRN number, invoice number and IRN, e-way bill number, payment reference) with a one-tap PDF in English plus the user's language. In the prototype, simulated escrow releases on the buyer's receipt (net weight after deductions).** (prototype-now): This is how factories already run a purchase (Tally: PO, then Receipt Note, then Purchase voucher), and competitors advertise the same 'invoice, e-way bill and weight certificate in one place'. It needs only optional fields on the existing trades table.
- **Lot traceability with a mass-balance check: lot IDs from kabadiwala sorting runs to yard bales to recycler input, a trace view back to household pickups, and a warning when a business sells more than it received or more than its declared capacity.** (prototype-now): The Karnataka case (certificates for 3.48 lakh tonnes from a plant that was not running) and CPCB's installed-capacity cap make this the credibility story for investors. ISCC PLUS and GRS audits also need lot-level evidence.
- **Factory team roles: the owner invites staff by phone number, and each gets a preset role (owner, purchase, gate/weighbridge, quality, accounts, compliance, plant) that opens on its own screen.** (prototype-now): A factory's work is spread across several people, but memberships currently only has owner and staff. A small enum change lets the demo show every role inside a factory.
- **Export centre. Now: CSV and Excel downloads laid out like Tally's sample import files for Purchase, Receipt Note and Sales vouchers (material code, English name, HSN, kg, rate, amount, party GSTIN), plus a monthly 'EPR procurement register' CSV in CPCB portal field order. Pilot: add Tally XML after testing with 2-3 real accountants.** (prototype-now): Tally already imports Excel, XML and JSON and reports errors before committing, and the CPCB fields are fixed. Exports need no API and can be built client-side or through a Convex HTTP action.
- **Share to WhatsApp and email: the Web Share API passes the slip or pack PDF and CSVs to WhatsApp on phones; on desktop, download plus a mailto link. wa.me text links for status updates.** (prototype-now): Free, no template approval, and it works in the installable web app today. WhatsApp is where yards and buyers already talk.
- **Gate and weighbridge screen (phone, Kannada first): weigh-in and weigh-out against an order, with vehicle number, gross/tare/net, slip photo and the scale's stamping-valid-until date. It compares the seller's and buyer's net weights and flags gaps over 1%.** (pilot): The ISRI paper rules settle disputes on certified-scale weights with a 1% domestic tolerance, and Bengaluru Legal Metrology finds many unstamped or tampered scales. Recording both weights plus the photo is the cheapest fraud control.
- **Quality check with deductions: parameters and default limits per material (paper moisture 12%; OCC prohibitives 1% and outthrows plus prohibitives 5%; plastics moisture, contamination and colour), with accept, deduct or reject. Deductions change the receipt weight or price, and the seller sees the reason and photos at once.** (pilot): Quality deductions are where money and trust are lost between yard and mill. Recording them as data also gives the investor story on grade quality.
- **Add fields to the material catalogue (data only, no code change): HSN code, GST unit code (e.g. KGS), EPR plastic category I to IV, a reference grade (ISRI/ReMA name), and per-organisation alias names ('raddi', buyer grade names).** (pilot): Invoices and Tally imports need HSN codes and units, the CPCB register needs the category, and buyers use their own grade names. The catalogue is already seeded from convex/lib/catalogue.ts, so this stays easy to change.
- **Tax and paperwork flags on each trade, with no filing: e-way bill needed (over ₹50,000; the buyer raises it when the seller is unregistered); metal-scrap reverse charge, self-invoice due within 30 days; GST TDS on metal scrap between registered parties above ₹2.5 lakh; TCS on scrap at the current rate. Rates and thresholds sit in an admin-editable table with effective dates, like price floors.** (pilot): The rules changed in Oct-Nov 2024 and again from Apr 2026 (TCS 1% to 2%, as proposed). Keeping them as dated data follows the founders' 'easy to change' rule and avoids tax-filing liability.
- **Compliance vault and reminders: consent number, colour category and validity (checked by the admin against the public KSPCB XGN register), EPR/PWP registration, installed capacity, the 30 April annual-return date, and document expiry alerts. One click builds an audit pack (all documents for a period, zipped).** (pilot): Compliance staff juggle the XGN and CPCB portals and folders of PDFs. The consent-validity data is public in Karnataka, which makes verification cheap.
- **Payables and receivables with the MSME 45-day clock: Udyam number on the organisation, due date set to the earlier of the agreed date and 45 days, overdue badges, and the payment reference recorded (money stays off-platform, per ADR 0009).** (pilot): Late payment is the MSME sector's largest dispute category (2.57 lakh cases, ₹31,690 crore). It matters to both yards and manufacturers and needs no payment licence.
- **Deeper integrations: a Tally connector (TDL add-on or local agent using Tally's XML/JSON HTTP interface), CSV over SFTP or an API for SAP/ERP plants, e-invoice and e-way bill generation through a GST Suvidha Provider (GSP), WhatsApp Business API utility templates billed in INR through a Meta partner, data exports for GRS and ISCC PLUS auditors, and a CPCB EPR portal feed if an API appears.** (scale): Each needs credentials, local installs or vendor contracts. They pay off only once factories trade on the platform regularly.

## Risks

- Tax rules change often: metal-scrap reverse charge and TDS arrived in Oct 2024, and TCS on scrap was proposed to double from 1 Apr 2026. Hard-coded logic or advice-like wording creates liability, so show flags with 'confirm with your CA' and keep rates as dated data.
- A platform record can be faked just as EPR invoices were. Without photo evidence, both parties confirming the weight, and mass-balance alerts, Luma.Green could end up laundering bad data instead of proving good data.
- Presenting Luma.Green's records as 'certificates' or trading EPR credits would clash with CPCB's authorisation of EPR trading platforms and with the GRS/ISCC certification bodies. Stay a data layer that certified parties draw on.
- A live Tally sync means installing and supporting a local agent on customers' PCs, a security and support burden for a small team, and Tally versions vary across customers. Exporting files avoids this.
- WhatsApp Business API adds per-message costs and template approvals, and Indian accounts must move to INR billing by 31 Dec 2026. WhatsApp groups themselves cannot be automated.
- CPCB asks for PAN or Aadhaar of unregistered suppliers. Storing kabadiwalas' Aadhaar numbers raises data-protection (DPDP) risk: collect only when a recycler needs it for EPR, mask it, and limit access to the compliance role.
- Requiring pollution-board consent screens out many of Bengaluru's small, unregistered recyclers, so early factory adoption may be thin. Start with registered yards and recyclers.
- Recykal Market and MetalMandi already offer document dashboards at scale. Without upstream origin data and Kannada-first weighing, Luma.Green looks like a late copy.
- Exports in Indian scripts can break matching against Tally masters, and Tally's document-reading AI (Docs by Ira) reads only English, non-handwritten documents. Always include material codes and English names in exports and PDFs.

## Open questions

- Which accounting software do the pilot yards and recyclers use (Tally, Busy, Marg, Excel), and which Tally release? This decides between Excel and XML exports.
- Do pilot yards weigh on their own stamped weighbridge, a public weighbridge or platform scales, and does their slip carry a serial number?
- What grade names, quality parameters and deduction rules do Bengaluru buyers write on POs today (ISRI names, mill specs, local names)?
- For metal, do pilot yards buy from unregistered kabadiwalas, and do they issue reverse-charge self-invoices today?
- Do pilot recyclers hold CPCB processor (PWP) registration, and does the portal accept bulk uploads from processors, or only the per-invoice 'Single Entry' form the manual shows?
- Will buyers, GRS/ISCC auditors or CPCB physical verification accept Luma.Green's weight and photo record as supporting evidence?
- What TCS rate on scrap did the Finance Act 2026 actually enact, what is Karnataka's intra-state e-way bill limit, and is the 45-day MSME payment rule kept under the Income-tax Act 2025?
- Which factory roles will actually log in during the pilot: only the owner, or also gate, quality and accounts staff?
- Should brand owners and auditors get read-only trace links in the investor demo, and who controls sharing them?
