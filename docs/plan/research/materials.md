# materials

_Research brief, 29 September 2026. Headline:_ India's customs tariff already uses ISRI grade names for scrap: 10 copper grades under 7404 00 12 and about 40 aluminium grades under 7602 00 10. So Luma.Green can publish a 60-code, two-tier catalogue that links doorstep words like raddi, gatta, Teri and Panni to 8-digit HSN lines, ISRI and IS 2549 grades and EPR categories, rather than inventing new grades.

## Luma Material Codes (LMC): proposed v0.1 catalogue (60 codes)

**How to read it**

- **Code**: 3-letter family plus a grade token, in ASCII. Codes are never renamed or reused; a retired code points to its replacement (`replacedBy`).
- **Level**: H = household (doorstep and AI photo estimate), T = trade (kabadiwala sorts for the yard), I = industrial (recycler output).
- **HSN**: the Indian tariff item. It is 8-digit where checked against the ITC-HS schedule; where it wasn't checked, it is 4- or 6-digit and marked (tbc).
- **Unit**: the pricing unit, with its GST unit code (kg = KGS, piece = NOS, dozen = DOZ, bale = BAL, tonne = MTS). Mass is always stored in grams.
- **Trade names**: hi Hindi, mr Marathi, kn Kannada, ta Tamil, gu Gujarati. _Delhi_ = names recorded by CSE (2021) in Delhi's informal plastics market. † = meaning checked in Wiktionary, the Alar dictionary or CSE; (tbc) = to confirm with the Bengaluru pilot kabadiwalas. Display names in all 12 languages come from `messages/*.json`; these trade names are search and voice aliases.
- **ISRI** = code words and paper grade numbers from the ISRI (now ReMA) Scrap Specifications Circular 2024.

**GST by family** (Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate), in force from 22 Sep 2025):

- 5%: paper (4707), glass cullet (7001), rags and worn clothing (6309/6310).
- 18%: plastics (3915), all metal scrap, e-waste (8549), batteries.
- FER- and NFE- codes fall under chapters 72–81. When an unregistered seller supplies a registered buyer, the buyer pays GST under reverse charge. Supplies between registered parties above Rs 2.5 lakh carry a 2% GST TDS (both since 10 Oct 2024).
- EWS- and BAT- codes can go only to CPCB or state-board registered recyclers and refurbishers.

Each code also carries: messageKey, aliases, unit and unit code, typical grams per piece, references, quality defaults, flags (shown to AI, chapter 72–81 metal, regulated), icon and colour.

## Recommendations

- **Publish 'Luma Material Codes v0.1': the 60-code catalogue as a public page in 12 languages, with JSON and CSV downloads** (prototype-now): It gives investors a concrete standard-setting asset within weeks. Each code links doorstep words (raddi, gatta, Teri) to an 8-digit HSN line, ISRI or IS 2549 grades and an EPR category. It is data rather than a new user flow, so it is low-risk to build, and every later module (prices, stock, exchange, carbon) reuses it. Version it as 0.1.0, date every row, use an open licence (for example CC BY 4.0) and state that the tax columns are for information only.
- **Two-tier catalogue with roll-up: household, trade and industrial codes linked by parentCode, with sorting entries that move kilograms between them** (prototype-now): The brief says names and prices change at each hand-off. Households and the AI need about 20–30 simple codes; yards buy ISRI and IS 2549 grades. The planned sorting entry then reads 'PAP-MIX 50 kg becomes PAP-ONP 30 + PAP-OCC 15 + reject 5', which makes a clear traceability demo.
- **Catalogue schema in Convex: immutable ASCII codes (3-letter family plus grade, for example PAP-OCC), with GST rates and CO2e factors kept in separate dated tables** (prototype-now): The fields are: code, family, level, parentCode, messageKey, aliases per locale, hsn, unit and UQC, typicalGramsPerPiece, variants, and references (ISRI code words, IS 2549 grade, EN 643 grade, EPR category, resin code, e-waste schedule code, battery type). Add quality defaults (moisture, prohibitives, outthrows, metal content, oil), flags (shown to the AI, metal scrap under chapters 72–81, regulated e-waste or battery, low value), icon, colour, active, replacedBy and catalogue version. The families would be paper, plastic, ferrous, nonferrous, glass, ewaste, battery, textile and other. Splitting metal into ferrous and non-ferrous and adding battery matches how buyers, tax (reverse charge only for chapters 72–81) and rules differ. Dated rate and factor tables follow ADR 0008, so a rate change never rewrites a receipt. One seed JSON file in the repo, plus admin editing of names, aliases and active flags, keeps the data easy to change.
- **Limit the AI estimate to about 20 household codes it can recognise in a photo, and have it return a 'we don't buy / low value' list (thermocol, multi-layer packets, food-soiled paper)** (prototype-now): A smaller set of answers should score better on the labelled photo set. Multi-layer packets fetch only Rs 4–5/kg, and food or medical waste is never accepted in paper bales, so households should be told early.
- **Bengaluru name-collection sprint: record the words 10 pilot kabadiwalas actually use for each code, in Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu and Hindi, with short voice clips** (pilot): Dictionaries contradict street usage (gujari, rattu), Delhi plastic slang may not be used in Bengaluru, and sound-alikes such as seesa (lead) and sheesha (glass) are a real risk when the app reads requests aloud. The collected words become aliases for search and the speaker button; display names stay hand-translated.
- **Per-piece e-waste and battery codes, with variants (fridge doors, AC tonnage, battery type) and a typical weight per piece measured during the pilot** (pilot): Appliances are priced per piece at the door (fridges Rs 1,000–1,800, ACs Rs 3,500–5,000), but stock, carbon and EPR reporting need kilograms. Recording both from day one avoids a later migration.
- **Quality and deduction lines at the kabadiwala-to-yard hand-off (moisture %, contamination %, recovery %), pre-filled from catalogue defaults such as 12% moisture for paper and the ISRI limits for OCC** (pilot): Weight is the source of truth, and deductions are where disputes and trust are decided. Standard defaults make prices comparable across yards and later feed the escrow module.
- **Tax-aware records: print the HSN code and unit code on receipts and delivery challans, and for ferrous and non-ferrous codes sold by an unregistered kabadiwala to a registered yard, show 'GST payable by buyer under reverse charge'** (pilot): Yards have needed these facts since 10 Oct 2024. Recording them without filing anything fits ADR 0009 (money off-platform first) and makes Luma useful to GST-registered buyers.
- **Route regulated streams: e-waste and battery codes can be offered only to businesses whose CPCB or state-board recycler or refurbisher registration the admin has verified** (pilot): The e-waste and battery rules require registered recyclers and refurbishers. Routing by code keeps Luma from facilitating illegal dismantling, and it becomes a trust feature.
- **EPR traceability export: tag each plastics lot with its EPR category, resin code and post-consumer origin, from household code to recycler output (for example PLA-PET to PLA-PET-FLAKE)** (scale): Recycled-content targets reach 60% for Category I by 2028-29, so brand owners and processors need traceable feedstock. The same pattern later serves e-waste and battery EPR and carbon credits.
- **Run the catalogue as an open standard: public change requests, semantic versioning, codes never reused, maintained crosswalks (HSN, ISRI, EN 643, IS 2549, e-waste schedule, battery categories), and an invitation to trade bodies such as IARPMA** (scale): The industry will adopt the codes only if they are stable and neutral. Tariffs and rules keep changing (GST in 2021 and 2025, plastic rules 2022–2026), so the crosswalks need a scheduled review.

## Risks

- Publishing HSN and GST columns invites people to rely on them for tax filings. A wrong 8-digit line could cause misclassification, and the 7001, 7802 and 8549 sub-lines are not yet checked. Mark these columns as for information only, date every row and have the admin review them.
- ISRI specifications are copyrighted (2024 ISRI). Reproducing their definitions in a public standard could draw objections, so cite the code words (which India's own tariff uses) and write original definitions.
- E-waste and battery handling by unregistered kabadiwalas is restricted. A marketplace that lists these items without routing them to registered recyclers could be seen as facilitating illegal dismantling.
- A wrong or offensive local name (seesa for lead vs sheesha for glass, or gujari) would erode trust with low-literacy users, and Delhi plastic slang may mislead in Bengaluru.
- There is a granularity trade-off. Too many codes overwhelm kabadiwalas and the AI; too few hide price spreads (copper at Rs 550 vs over Rs 1,000/kg) and mislead households.
- Rules keep changing: GST in 2021 and 2025, plastic rules from 2022 to 2026, battery rules from 2023 to 2025. Without dated tables and a review schedule, static data will go out of date.
- Renaming or reusing codes would corrupt receipts, stock history and EPR or carbon claims, so codes must be immutable.
- Showing reverse-charge and TDS facts may push informal traders to under-record transactions, or create an expectation that Luma gives tax advice.

## Open questions

- What are the exact 8-digit lines for glass (7001 00 xx), lead (7802 00 xx), the 8549 sub-headings (lithium-ion, dry cells, CRT glass, circuit boards), 6309/6310 and reusable bottles (7010)? Confirm against the CBIC tariff before publishing.
- Which e-waste schedule item codes (the ITEW, CEEW and LSEEW numbers) match each appliance code? This needs the official schedule text.
- What words do Bengaluru kabadiwalas use for plastics: Delhi's Teri, Phugga, Jhaap and Panni, English PET, PP, HD and LD, or Kannada terms? Are milk pouches (Nandini) a separate paid stream?
- Is 'gujari' used for scrap shops in Bengaluru, given that the dictionary defines it as a small retail or evening market?
- Should small appliances and fans be priced per kg (Delhi cards pay Rs 25/kg for fans) or per piece?
- Will the pilot show tax facts at all, given that ADR 0009 keeps money off-platform first?
- Did new national solid-waste rules (reported for 2026, not verified here) add a separate household stream for hazardous items that should own batteries and bulbs?
- Who owns the standard in the long term (Luma alone or a group of industry partners), and under which licence?
- Which source should supply the emission factor for each code? That is for the carbon module's research.
