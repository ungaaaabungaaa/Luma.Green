# India entity trademark and legal preparation

Prepared 2 October 2026 for the Luma.Green founder. The founder confirmed that
an Indian entity has not yet been registered. This is a preparation checklist
for the founder, a company secretary or chartered accountant, and legal counsel.
It is not a filing, a trademark clearance opinion or an approved customer contract.
No registry search, entity incorporation or trademark application was completed
by this software task. Keep identity papers and signed documents outside Git.

## Decisions before accounts and contracts

Choose the legal owner before opening business accounts or signing customer and
provider agreements. Record the proposed entity type, proposed legal names,
registered office, founders, ownership, directors or partners, authorised signer,
and the owner of the domain, code and brand. Consider funding plans, governance,
liability, tax and ongoing compliance with the professional who will file.

For a company, MCA provides SPICe+ and linked incorporation forms. Check current
requirements in the official portal; a company-name reservation is separate
from trademark clearance. An LLP requires its own route and documents. Do not
publish an invented legal name, CIN, GSTIN or registered-office address while
formation is pending. [MCA incorporation guidance](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/dam/mca/pdf/SPICEplus-and-linked-filings-FAQs-V3-20230122.pdf)
and [MCA portal](https://mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/home.html).

Do not assume that a software marketplace has the same obligations as a
recycling plant, transporter, waste processor or payment operator. Write down
which activities Luma.Green itself will perform and which are partner services.
Ask counsel and the CA to assess the actual model, including marketplace tax,
invoicing, consumer obligations and labour arrangements. No applicability or
exemption is established by this document.

## Trademark work packet

Use separate work items for the **Luma.Green word mark** and the **pinwheel logo**.
The domain name and a free social handle do not establish trademark rights.

1. Decide the proposed proprietor. If a founder files before incorporation,
   have counsel plan ownership and any later assignment to the entity.
2. Search exact, spaced and phonetic variants, including Luma Green, Luma.Green
   and Luma. Review similar names, devices and relevant goods or services.
   Keep a dated search report and the examiner's or counsel's risk assessment.
3. Describe actual services and intended use. Ask counsel to choose the class
   scope. Candidate areas for review are an online marketplace, hosted software
   and downloadable applications; separately assess logistics or material
   processing only if the business will provide those services itself.
4. Supply the mark artwork, proprietor details, use basis, first-use evidence
   if applicable, and any agent authority or eligibility proof requested.
5. File the approved specification through the official TM-A workflow. Record
   the application number, acknowledgement, class, proprietor and filing date.
6. Assign one owner to monitor examination, objections, publication, opposition
   and renewal. Record deadlines from actual registry notices. Do not use the
   registration symbol before the relevant registration is granted.

IP India lists e-filing fees of **₹4,500 per mark per class** for an eligible
individual, startup or small enterprise, and **₹9,000** in other cases.
Professional work and later proceedings are separate. Confirm eligibility and
fees at filing; this is not a fee quote. [Official forms and fees](https://www.ipindia.gov.in/pages/trade-marks/learn/forms-and-official-fees).
The [official filing workflow](https://ipindia.gov.in/application-workflow/trademark-filing-process)
and [public search services](https://ipindia.gov.in/pages/e-services) are the
starting points. Counsel should verify the current
[WIPO goods and services classification](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/how_to/file/madrid-system-filing-international-trademark-applications-classification-of-goods-and-services).

## Data protection work before the pilot

MeitY publishes the DPDP Rules 2025 and a separate enforcement timeline.
Have counsel map the duties and their applicable dates to this business before
launch; do not treat the presence of a consent switch as compliance.
[Official rules and timeline](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digit).

Prepare a data inventory for phone numbers, household addresses, location pins,
business certificates, identity uploads, receipts, audit records and request
counters. Distinguish the intended retention policy from implemented deletion.
The existing [data protection draft](data-protection.md) requires legal and
implementation review; its retention and response targets are not proof that
automated erasure exists.

Check the optional AI photo route against the selected provider's actual
retention and processing terms. The application does not save scrap photos;
provider transmission still requires clear notice and controlled configuration.
Use masked or alternative identity documents for Saathi review. Test rejection
and removal handling for excessive data without keeping copies in test reports.
Document account access, correction and erasure requests, incident triage,
backup expiry, vendor exports and the limits imposed by required recordkeeping.

## SMS and environmental records

Before operational SMS, complete the principal-entity, header and template
registration and mapping required for the selected route. Include the intended
languages and booking links. Confirm handset delivery and provider failures;
an API key alone is not approval. [TRAI sender guidance](https://www.trai.gov.in/advice-to-senders)
and [MSG91 registration guide](https://web.msg91.com/guide/steps-for-dlt-process-registration).

The CPCB plastic portal states that its operations moved to the Common EPR
Portal on 28 June 2026. Use the current portal and the relevant authority to
verify each partner's applicable registration, consent and permitted materials.
Luma.Green's uploaded document check does not grant a licence or replace that
verification. [CPCB migration notice](https://www.eprplastic.cpcb.gov.in/)
and [Common EPR Portal](https://epr.cpcb.gov.in/).

## Release record

For each document, record version, owner, reviewer, approval date, effective
date, where the user sees it and the evidence of acceptance when required.
Keep approved customer text separate from internal drafts. Recheck documents
when the entity, data provider, retention behavior, payment model, material
scope, geography or worker arrangement changes. The founder signs off the
business model; qualified advisers review legal and tax applicability.
