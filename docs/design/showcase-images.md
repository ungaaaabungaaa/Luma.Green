## Processing and review

Sharp 0.35.4 converted the generated PNG outputs to WebP with `effort: 6`, a maximum width of 1536 pixels and `withoutEnlargement: true`. Quality started at 78 and decreased in steps of five until the result was at most 180,000 bytes. No crop, compositing, text overlay or scene alteration was applied. Sharp's default metadata stripping was retained.

The first four originals and final WebP files were visually inspected during generation. The four additional WebP files were inspected in the continuation review. Subjects and materials are clear. The images share natural light, forest green and ivory tones. No customer details, readable brand labels, app interfaces or certification marks appear. The yard's compressed cans contain small colour fragments; they are material texture, not a brand endorsement. The workbench caliper includes ordinary measurement marks. The shop scale has small unreadable display marks. The operations laptop has an abstract green wallpaper, with no application or record content. No material visual defect was found. No existing project asset was overwritten.

The source PNG mapping and quality settings for the four additional files were verified by repeating the Sharp conversion in memory and comparing the output bytes with each committed WebP. All four matched exactly. The asset test enforces 180,000 bytes per file and 1,440,000 bytes for the complete set.
